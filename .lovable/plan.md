

## Kongu Engineering College & GUVI Website

A modern, professional multi-page website showcasing information about Kongu Engineering College and GUVI company, including courses, events, placements, and career opportunities.

---

### Pages & Structure

**1. Home Page**
- Hero section with combined branding of both institutions
- Quick overview of Kongu Engineering College
- Quick overview of GUVI company
- Highlights of the partnership/collaboration
- Call-to-action buttons for key sections

**2. About Page**
- Detailed information about Kongu Engineering College (history, vision, mission)
- Detailed information about GUVI (company overview, what they do)
- Key achievements and milestones

**3. Courses & Programs Page**
- List of courses/certifications offered by GUVI
- Programs available at Kongu Engineering College
- Course details with descriptions

**4. Events Page**
- Upcoming and past events showcase
- Event cards with dates, descriptions, and images
- Workshops, seminars, and hackathons

**5. Placements & Careers Page**
- Placement statistics (students placed, top companies, average package)
- Job listings section with positions, requirements, and apply buttons
- Internship opportunities
- Success stories/testimonials

**6. Contact Page**
- Contact form (name, email, phone, message, inquiry type)
- Contact information for both organizations
- Embedded map showing locations
- Social media links

---

### Design & Features

- **Modern professional design** with clean layouts and subtle animations
- **Responsive navigation** with mobile-friendly menu
- **Consistent color scheme** inspired by both organizations
- **Form validation** for the contact/application form
- **Statistics counters** for placement highlights
- **Card-based layouts** for courses, events, and job listings
